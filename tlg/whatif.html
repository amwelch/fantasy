How it works

  • Points are what each player actually scored that week; only lineups change.
  • Replacements can be any position — starters shift slots to keep the lineup legal.
  • Each scenario undoes one trade on its own. Draft picks in the trades are ignored.
  • Max PF leaves out each team's current taxi squad, so it can differ from Sleeper's by a few points.
