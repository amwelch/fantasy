How the simulation works

  • Each scenario undoes one trade. The page has one scenario for each trade in which a team sent away its own 2027 1st-round pick.
  • The simulation ignores the draft picks in each trade.
  • Each player gets the points that he actually scored that week. Only the lineups change.
  • The original team keeps each traded player for the full season. This rule also applies if the receiving team later traded or dropped the player.
  • Other players and picks in a later trade stay where that trade sent them.
  • If a team loses a starter, the team starts its highest-projected bench player. The bench player can play any position. The other starters move to different slots if the lineup needs it.
  • A returning player starts if his projection is higher than the projection of the lowest-projected starter that he can replace. Ashton Jeanty is the exception. He starts every week for amwelch.
  • Max PF does not include each team's current taxi squad, because the database does not record past taxi squads. Thus max PF can be different from the Sleeper value by a few points.
